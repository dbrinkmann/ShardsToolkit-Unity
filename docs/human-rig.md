# The human rig

Skeleton and bind-pose data of the base game's human bodies, for authoring wearables. See [wearables.md](wearables.md).

Taken from the game client's `Prefabs/Mobiles/HumanMaleObject` (ClientId 1) and `Prefabs/Mobiles/HumanFemaleObject` (ClientId 208).
Units are metres. The model faces +Z, Y is up, and the character's left is -X. "Model space" is relative to the `Model` object, which sits at the prefab root with no rotation or scale.

## 1. Bind pose of the skinned bones

These are the bones the body meshes are skinned to, in the pose the meshes were bound in. Every body mesh agrees exactly. **The male is bound in a T-pose and the female in an A-pose** (arms about 40 degrees down).

Columns: model-space position (x, y, z) and model-space rotation as Unity euler angles (x, y, z). Rebuild the skeleton in your 3D package from these numbers and section 2, then check each skinned bone's bind position against this table before skinning. Small mistakes show as stretched or offset clothing in game.
